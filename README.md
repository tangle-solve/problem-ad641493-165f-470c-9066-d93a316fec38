### ALGO-000194: Subquadratic three-query relaxed locally decodable codes
- **Subfield:** Quantum complexity and locally decodable codes
- **Open:** Construct constant-alphabet three-query relaxed locally decodable codes of length \(O(k^{2-\varepsilon})\) for some fixed \(\varepsilon>0\), or prove that near-quadratic length is necessary.
- **Status:** Gur–Minzer–Weissenberg–Zheng’s December 2025 construction achieves \(\widetilde O(k^2)\). General lower bounds do not settle this exact exponent.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines how much allowing an explicit “failure” output reduces local-decoding redundancy.
- **Key works:** Gur et al. (2025), *3-Query RLDCs Are Strictly Stronger than 3-Query LDCs*, arXiv:2512.12960; Goldberg, Gur, and Saraogi (2025), arXiv:2511.21659.
